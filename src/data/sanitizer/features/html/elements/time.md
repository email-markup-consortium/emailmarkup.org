## Attributes

- `datetime`

## Recommendation

It is highly recommended for an email client to support the `<time>` element and its attributes.

## Impacts

### Accessibility

Preserving `datetime` retains an unambiguous machine-readable date or time alongside the visible text. Its use by assistive technology varies, so visible text should remain understandable on its own.
