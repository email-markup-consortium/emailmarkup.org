## Attributes

This element has no element-specific attributes.

## Recommendation

It is highly recommended for an email client to support the `<wbr>` element.

## Impacts

### Internationalisation

Preserving optional break opportunities helps long words and identifiers fit narrow email layouts while retaining their textual content.
