## Attributes

- `align` - deprecated

## Recommendation

It is highly recommended for an email client to support the `<caption>` element.

### Deprecated attributes

The positive recommendation does not include the deprecated attributes listed above. Their handling is under active discussion.

## Impacts

### Accessibility

Keeping a caption associated with its table helps assistive technology identify the table and its purpose. A caption should remain inside its original table.
