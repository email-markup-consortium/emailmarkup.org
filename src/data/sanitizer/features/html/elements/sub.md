## Attributes

This element has no element-specific attributes.

## Recommendation

It is highly recommended for an email client to support the `<sub>` element.

## Impacts

### Accessibility

Subscripts can be essential to the meaning of formulas and identifiers. Preserve their relationship to the surrounding text; complex mathematical expressions may need MathML for accessible structure.
